# Project Proposal Guide

The project proposal serves two purposes:

1. It helps your team develop a clear and feasible plan for the term project.
2. It allows me to review your project early and provide feedback before you invest significant time in implementation.

Your proposal does not need to contain final results. At this stage, I am looking for a well-defined machine learning problem and a reasonable plan for completing and evaluating the project.

Please review the [Term Project Overview](./project-overview.md) before preparing your proposal.

---

## Format

Your proposal should be **no more than 3 pages, excluding references**.

Please prepare your proposal using **Overleaf** and the official **IEEE Conference Template**:

- [IEEE Conference Template on Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/ieee-conference-template/grfzhhncsfqn)

If you do not already have an Overleaf account, create a **free Overleaf account** at:

- [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/)

Use the IEEE conference template and keep the general IEEE formatting, including the two-column layout, fonts, margins, and citation style.

Please organize your proposal using the following sections.

---

---

## 2. Introduction

Briefly describe:

- The application or research problem.
- Why the problem is important or interesting.
- The machine learning setting, such as supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised, or reinforcement learning.
- The main goal of the project.
- A brief overview of your proposed approach.

---

## 3. Related Work

Briefly discuss prior work related to your project.

- Have similar problems been studied before?
- What methods have been used?
- How is your proposed project related to or different from existing work?

Include relevant academic references.

---

## 4. Data or Learning Environment

Describe the data or environment you plan to use.

For a data-based project, explain:

- What dataset will you use?
- Where does the data come from?
- What is the approximate size of the dataset?
- What preprocessing do you expect to perform?

If you are using the recommended **NHTSA CRSS** dataset, describe the subset and variables relevant to your project.

For a reinforcement learning project, describe the environment, states or observations, actions, and rewards.

---

## 5. Proposed Method

Describe the machine learning methods you plan to investigate.

Include:

- The baseline method.
- The main machine learning methods.
- Any important preprocessing or feature engineering.
- Software or libraries you expect to use.

You are not expected to have finalized every modeling decision at the proposal stage.

---

## 6. Evaluation Plan

Describe how you will evaluate your approach.

Include:

- The evaluation metrics or criteria.
- How you will compare methods fairly.
- For supervised learning, your planned training, validation, and test strategy.
- Potential issues such as data leakage, class imbalance, limited labeled data, or other methodological challenges.

---

## 7. Timeline and Team Responsibilities

Briefly describe:

- Major project milestones.
- The responsibilities of each team member.
- How your team plans to collaborate.

A short table is acceptable.

---

## 8. References

List all references using the IEEE citation style.
---

## Grading

The proposal will be graded out of 100 points.


| Item | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Introduction and problem formulation | 15 |
| Related work | 15 |
| Data or learning environment | 15 |
| Proposed method | 25 |
| Evaluation plan | 20 |
| Timeline and team responsibilities | 5 |
| Writing quality, IEEE formatting, and references | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

The proposal should demonstrate that your team has researched the problem and developed a **clear, meaningful, and feasible plan** for the remainder of the semester.

I recognize that your project may change as you learn more about the problem and conduct experiments. The proposal is an initial plan, not a contract.

---

## Delivery

Submit your project proposal as a **PDF file to Blackboard**.

The proposal should be prepared using the **IEEE Conference Template in Overleaf**.

Only **one submission per team** is required.

All components of the term project are subject to GVSU and the School of Computing's academic honesty policies.
