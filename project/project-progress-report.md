# Project Progress Report

The project progress report is an opportunity to summarize what your team has completed, present preliminary machine learning results, identify challenges, and describe your plan for completing the project.

The purpose of this report is not to repeat your project proposal. Instead, focus on **what you have actually accomplished since the proposal** and what remains to be done.

Please review the [Term Project Overview](./project-overview.md) and your approved project proposal before preparing this report.

---

## Format

Your progress report should be approximately **1–2 pages, excluding references**.

Please prepare the report using **Overleaf** and the official **IEEE Conference Template**:

- [IEEE Conference Template on Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/ieee-conference-template/grfzhhncsfqn)

If you do not already have an Overleaf account, create a **free Overleaf account** at:

- [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/)

Keep the general IEEE formatting, including the two-column layout, fonts, margins, and citation style.

Use your **project title** as the paper title and list **all team members as authors**.

Please organize the report using the following sections.

## 2. Project Progress

Briefly summarize the current status of your project.

Describe:

- What parts of the project have been completed?
- Has the project problem or goal changed since the proposal?
- What data have you obtained and prepared?
- What preprocessing or data cleaning has been completed?
- What baseline or machine learning methods have been implemented?

Focus on completed work rather than repeating the original project plan.

---

## 3. Preliminary Experiments and Results

Describe the experiments you have completed so far.

Depending on your project, include:

- The baseline results.
- Machine learning methods tested so far.
- Current training, validation, or evaluation setup.
- Preliminary quantitative or qualitative results.
- Initial observations from the experiments.

Use tables or figures when they help communicate your progress clearly.

For supervised learning projects, include appropriate validation results rather than repeatedly evaluating on the final test set.

For unsupervised, semi-supervised, reinforcement learning, or other projects, report preliminary results using evaluation methods appropriate for your learning setting.

The results do **not** need to be final.

---

## 4. Challenges

Describe any important challenges encountered during the project.

Examples include:

- data quality or missing data,
- data leakage,
- class imbalance,
- limited labeled data,
- computational limitations,
- model implementation problems,
- unexpected experimental results,
- difficulty selecting evaluation metrics,
- or other technical or methodological issues.

Briefly explain:

- how you addressed the challenges, or
- how you plan to address unresolved challenges.

---

## 5. Next Steps

Describe the remaining work needed to complete the project.

Include:

- experiments that still need to be conducted,
- additional models or methods to evaluate,
- hyperparameter tuning,
- additional analysis,
- model interpretation or error analysis,
- and other major remaining tasks.

Provide a realistic plan for completing the project before the final report deadline.

---

## 6. Team Collaboration

Briefly describe:

- how your team has been collaborating,
- the contribution of each team member so far,
- and how the remaining work will be divided.

If there are any collaboration issues, explain how your team plans to address them.

---

## Grading

The progress report will be graded out of **100 points**.

| Item | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Project progress | 25 |
| Preliminary experiments and results | 30 |
| Challenges | 15 |
| Next steps | 15 |
| Team collaboration | 5 |
| Writing quality and IEEE formatting | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

### Grading Expectations

- **Project progress:** Clearly describes meaningful work completed since the proposal.
- **Preliminary experiments and results:** Presents appropriate initial experiments, baselines, results, and observations.
- **Challenges:** Identifies important technical or methodological challenges and explains how they are being addressed.
- **Next steps:** Provides a clear and feasible plan for completing the project.
- **Team collaboration:** Clearly describes each team member's contribution and the plan for remaining work.
- **Writing quality and IEEE formatting:** The report is concise, clear, professional, well organized, and follows the IEEE conference template.

The progress report should demonstrate that your team has made **substantial progress toward a complete machine learning project**.

Submit your progress report as a **PDF file to Blackboard**.
